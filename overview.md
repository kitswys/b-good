# 📊 B-good: Preferential Procurement Scorecard

## 🚀 Executive Summary
B-good is a specialized Odoo ERP module that turns preferential procurement from
a manual compliance burden into a live, decision-ready control system. It
automates B-BBEE procurement tracking, supplier evidence management, and score
visibility so leaders can see risk, protect spend, and improve outcomes in real
time.

Instead of chasing spreadsheets and end-of-month surprises, teams get a
predictive commercial view of compliance, score movement, and the next best
action.

## 🎯 Who This Is For
- **Financial Directors & CFOs:** Track **Spend At Risk** and understand how
  supplier compliance moves corporate point thresholds, procurement value, and
  commercial exposure.
- **Procurement & Supply Chain Managers:** Run an operational control center
  that reveals non-compliant vendors instantly before buying decisions are
  finalized.
- **Compliance & B-BBEE Officers:** Use an audit-ready, automated system that
  removes spreadsheet dependence and keeps verification readiness continuously
  visible.

## 🛠️ Core Module Features
- **Preferential Procurement Dashboard:** An executive cockpit with live
  B-BBEE level calculation, total point tracking, and automated target-scoring
  indicators.
- **Intelligent Performance Diagnostics:** Clear, text-driven **What This
  Means** summaries that explain audit readiness and map the **Fastest
  Improvement Path** to the next scoring level.
- **Operational Compliance Forecast:** A secondary intelligence panel that
  highlights upcoming supplier document expirations and top-risk vendors with
  exposure metrics.
- **Supplier Profile Intelligence:** A centralized vendor matrix showing
  B-BBEE levels, recognition percentages, and documentation health at a glance.
- **Automated Evidence Packs:** A controlled document deck for certificates
  and affidavits, with native upload validation to support audit readiness.

## 💼 Why Choose B-good? (Business Value)
- **Eliminate Spreadsheet Chaos:** Automates the extraction of posted vendor
  bills and matches them dynamically against live supplier compliance states.
- **Protect Procurement Spend:** Surfaces Rand value that is vulnerable to
  auditor rejection because of expired or missing supplier paperwork.
- **Proactive Risk Mitigation:** Moves the business from year-end compliance
  panic to continuous, predictive, daily monitoring.

## ⚙️ How It Works (The 3-Step Flow)
1. **Ingest & Extract:** The module syncs with posted Odoo vendor bills to
   capture total in-scope procurement spend.
2. **Analyze & Score:** B-good cross-references vendor data against active,
   verified certificates and affidavits.
3. **Predict & Optimize:** The B-good engine updates the scorecard, isolates
   point gaps, and outputs clear strategic actions.
