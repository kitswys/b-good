Product Documentation

How B-good works inside Odoo

The module is designed as a procurement control layer across Contacts, Purchase, Accounting, Attachments, and Reporting. It supports operational decisions without replacing accredited B-BBEE verification.

Supplier Compliance Procurement Spend Score Visibility Evidence Packs Security Model
01

Supplier Compliance Register

Vendor records are extended with a B-BBEE profile that captures level, recognition percentage, certificate or affidavit type, document reference, issue and expiry dates, ownership percentages, and empowering supplier status.

  • Active, expired, expiring soon, missing, and superseded evidence states.
  • Document attachments stored against the supplier profile for audit traceability.
  • Buyer-facing warnings when supplier evidence creates procurement risk.
02

Procurement Spend Snapshot

B-good imports posted vendor bills and credit notes into measurement-period batches. The imported lines store supplier, transaction date, untaxed amount, included amount, exclusion amount, recognition percentage, and evidence status at the time of calculation.

Recognised Spend = Included Spend x Supplier Recognition Percentage
03

Procurement Score Visibility

Scorecard assessments combine spend batches with a configurable rule set. Teams can monitor measured spend, recognised procurement spend, target percentage, achieved percentage, available points, actual points, and shortfall.

04

Verification Evidence Packs

Evidence packs collect supplier certificates, spend schedules, missing-evidence reports, exception registers, audit logs, and summary reports for a selected measurement period.

  • Versioned packs support internal review and final verification preparation.
  • Frozen packs preserve the state used for a verification discussion.
  • Missing documents remain visible rather than being hidden by aggregate totals.
05

Whitepaper 01 · Procurement Governance

From Year-End Scramble to Continuous Procurement Control

Many organisations treat B-BBEE procurement reporting as a year-end exercise. Finance exports spend, procurement searches for certificates, consultants request evidence, and teams discover too late that supplier documents have expired or that recognised spend has fallen behind target.

Continuous procurement control changes the operating model. Supplier evidence is captured when supplier data is maintained. Recognition percentages are visible when purchase decisions are made. Accounting transactions are imported into measurement periods while there is still time to adjust buying behaviour.

The result is not automatic certification. It is better governance: clear ownership, reproducible calculations, visible exceptions, and a documented route from source transaction to recognised procurement spend.

Whitepaper 02 · Evidence Management

Why Supplier Certificates Need a Lifecycle, Not a Folder

A shared folder can store certificates, but it cannot reliably answer whether a document was valid on the date spend occurred. B-good treats evidence as lifecycle data: uploaded, active, expiring, expired, superseded, and preserved for review.

This matters because verification conversations depend on timing and traceability. A certificate that is valid today may not support a transaction from a prior period, and a superseded document may still be relevant for audit history.

Whitepaper 03 · Finance Controls

The Case for Snapshotting Accounting Spend

B-BBEE reporting should not mutate posted accounting records. Instead, B-good imports a snapshot into a procurement spend batch. The snapshot stores the supplier, source transaction, date, amount, classification, and recognition percentage used for the assessment.

This protects accounting integrity while giving transformation and procurement teams a working dataset for classification, exclusions, evidence review, and score visibility.

Whitepaper 04 · Buying Decisions

Procurement Warnings Should Inform, Not Block

Buyers need context before confirming RFQs and purchase orders. A supplier may have expired evidence, missing documents, low recognition, or incomplete profile data. B-good surfaces these risks at the point of purchase.

The system should warn and inform, while still preserving business discretion. Where policy allows an override, the reason should be captured so exceptions become visible governance data rather than informal decisions.

Whitepaper 05 · Verification Preparation

What Makes an Evidence Pack Useful?

A useful pack does more than collect files. It links the measurement period, supplier list, valid certificates, spend schedule, recognition-level summary, missing-evidence report, manual exclusions, and audit notes into one reviewable package.

B-good structures this pack so internal teams can see what is ready, what requires review, and what needs a documented exception before engaging a verification professional.

Supplier register setup

Phase 2

Spend import and review

Phase 3

Score and evidence readiness

Important Disclaimer

B-good supports procurement control. It does not certify B-BBEE status.

Final B-BBEE verification and certification remain the responsibility of accredited B-BBEE verification professionals, legal advisers, and qualified transformation specialists. The module is designed to improve operational visibility, evidence management, internal reporting, and verification preparation.
