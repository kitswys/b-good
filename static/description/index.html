What this module does

  • Tracks supplier B-BBEE profiles, ownership data, certificates, and affidavits.
  • Imports procurement spend from posted vendor bills and credit notes.
  • Calculates recognised spend, scorecard progress, and compliance risk.

Best for

  • Procurement teams managing supplier compliance
  • B-BBEE officers and consultants preparing audit packs
  • Finance leaders who need real-time recognised spend visibility

Dashboard Supplier Profiles Certificates Spend Batches Spend Lines Scorecard Assessments Evidence Packs Documentation

Installation

  1. Copy the addon into your Odoo addons path.
  2. Update the Apps list and install B-good.
  3. Load the demo data to see a guided scenario immediately.
